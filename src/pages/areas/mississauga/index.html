ReadyFix is a Toronto handyman service, and Mississauga is one of the nearby cities we also cover. Worth bundling

Services worth booking together in Mississauga

Because Mississauga visits are grouped by day, these are the jobs that make the most sense to combine into one trip.

  • Punch lists

    A full inspection or pre-listing list is the ideal Mississauga visit: everything quoted together and worked through room by room in one day.

  • Drywall repair

    Settling cracks in newer subdivisions and patches after plumbing access, finished and primed in a single visit with fast-setting compound.

  • TV & artwork mounting

    City Centre condos have concrete and steel-stud walls. We bring the right anchors so the TV, mirrors and shelves go up on the first trip.

  • Curtain rod installation

    Moving into a condo or new house usually means rods for every room. Doing them all at once is far easier than one window at a time.

  • Cabinet repair

    Builder-grade hinges and drawer slides wear out quickly. Doing the whole kitchen in one go makes the most of the visit.

  • Doors & trim

    Post-war homes in Applewood and Mineola have doors and trim that have shifted over the years. We adjust, re-secure and re-caulk while we're there.
