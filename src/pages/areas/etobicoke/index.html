From the lakeshore to the Kingsway

Etobicoke's housing changes a lot from south to north. Along Lake Shore Boulevard, Mimico, New Toronto and Long Branch have older, modest houses, many added onto over the years, alongside newer townhouses and infill. Humber Bay Shores is almost all condo towers. Inland, The Kingsway and Humber Valley Village are known for period homes from the 1920s to the 1940s, with plaster walls, solid wood doors and original trim. Further north and west, streets of post-war bungalows and 1950s–60s split-levels fill much of central Etobicoke.

Each of those calls for a slightly different approach. In older homes, we drill plaster carefully, look for solid backing and try to keep original trim and doors in service rather than replace them. In split-levels, it's the short stair runs, the doors between levels and the cracks where one level meets the next. In the Humber Bay towers, it's concrete, elevator bookings and building rules.

How our services fit Etobicoke homes

  • Doors & trim

    Heavy solid doors in Kingsway-era homes sag on worn hinges. We re-secure, adjust and ease them, and close up gaps in original baseboard and casing.

  • Drywall repair

    Split-levels crack where levels meet, and lakeshore additions crack where old meets new. We re-tape, feather and texture-match so repairs disappear under paint.

  • Curtain rod installation

    Humber Bay Shores condos have big windows and concrete above them. We anchor rods and ceiling tracks into concrete so they stay level for good.

  • Cabinet repair

    Bungalow and split-level kitchens are often decades old but well built. New hinges, slides and adjustments give them years more use.

  • TV & artwork mounting

    Plaster, drywall, brick or concrete: Etobicoke has all four. We mount to solid backing with the right anchors for the wall in front of us.

  • Small jobs

    Loose handrails on split-level stairs, closet doors off their track, a towel bar coming out of the wall: send the whole list for one visit.

FAQ

Etobicoke handyman questions

Can you match the original trim in an older Kingsway house?

Sometimes. Many older profiles are no longer stock, so we look for the closest match and source it at cost, or reuse and repair the original pieces where we can. Send a close-up photo of the trim, ideally with a tape measure across it.

Do you work in Humber Bay Shores condos?

Yes. We confirm elevator bookings with management, protect hallways and floors, and use concrete anchors for rods and mounts. Check your building's rules on drilling and working hours before the visit.

What do split-level homes usually need?

The usual list is doors between levels that rub or won't latch, loose handrails on the short stair runs, and cracks at the corners and seams where one level meets the next. All of those can usually be handled in one visit.

Do you cover north Etobicoke too?

Yes. We book jobs across Etobicoke, from Long Branch and Mimico up through Islington and Richview to Rexdale and Thistletown.
