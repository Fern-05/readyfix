Newer homes, common fixes

The services that suit Markham homes

  • Drywall repair

    Nail pops and hairline seam cracks are typical in newer houses as framing settles. We reset fasteners, re-tape seams and leave everything paint-ready.

  • Cabinet repair

    Builder-grade hinges and slides are often the first thing to go. We replace and adjust them, and can upgrade the whole kitchen to soft-close.

  • TV & artwork mounting

    Great rooms and open-concept main floors are made for big TVs and large art. We find the framing, mount level and keep cables tidy.

  • Curtain rod installation

    Tall windows and wide patio doors need long rods with proper centre supports. In condos, brackets often go into concrete.

  • Punch lists

    Selling, renting out or just moved in: one list, one quote, and one visit on our Markham day, with photos for owners who aren't local.

  • Doors & trim

    Doors that drift open or won't latch, and baseboard that has pulled away as the house settles. We adjust, re-secure and caulk.

FAQ

Markham handyman questions

Do you cover all of Markham?

Yes, from Thornhill and Milliken Mills to Unionville, Markham Village and Cornell. We group Markham visits on set days, so sending your list early helps us fit you into the next one.

My house is still under the builder's warranty. Should I call you?

Check with your builder first. Defects covered by the warranty should go through them so you don't lose that coverage. For anything outside it, like mounting, upgrades or wear and tear, we're happy to quote.

Can you work for a landlord who doesn't live in Markham?

Yes. We can coordinate access with your tenant, a lockbox or the building, send before and after photos of each item, and invoice online.

Is the price different outside Toronto?

Every job gets a written quote before work starts, and nothing is added without your approval. Grouping visits by day keeps the trip efficient, and bundling several items into one visit lowers the cost per item. See how our pricing works.
