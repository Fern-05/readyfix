Repairs that suit East York's post-war homes

East York was its own borough until the 1998 amalgamation, and a lot of its housing went up in the years after the Second World War: solid brick bungalows and storey-and-a-half houses on quiet streets around Pape Village, Woodbine Heights and O'Connor–Parkview. Leaside's brick houses are a little older, and many have been renovated more than once. Thorncliffe Park, Flemingdon Park and Crescent Town are mostly apartment towers.

Houses of this age tend to need the same things. Interior doors stick after decades of settling. Trim has gaps and tired caulk. Finished basements take knocks and the odd leak. Kitchens have cabinets on their second or third set of hinges. And because many East York houses have a basement apartment, turnover repairs between tenants come up often.

Services East York homes call us for

  • Doors & trim

    Bungalow doors and frames move as a house settles. We tighten hinges, adjust strikes, plane where needed and re-caulk trim so doors close cleanly again.

  • Drywall repair

    Finished basements are where most East York drywall damage happens. Once any leak is fixed, we replace damaged board, stain-block and leave it paint-ready.

  • Cabinet repair

    Older kitchens and basement-unit kitchenettes wear out hinges and slides first. Replacing them is far cheaper than new cabinets and fixes most sagging doors.

  • Lock replacement

    With so many basement apartments, deadbolts and handlesets get swapped between tenants. We fit the new hardware and align the strike so it latches every time.

  • Punch lists

    Leaside and East York houses draw careful buyers. We clear inspection items and pre-listing touch-ups so the house shows well and the deal stays on track.

  • Small jobs

    Loose handrails, wobbly towel bars, closet doors off their track: the list every older house builds up. Send it all and we'll quote one visit.

FAQ

East York handyman questions

Do you do turnover repairs for East York basement apartments?

Yes. Patching, door and cabinet fixes, lock changes and general touch-ups between tenants are a good fit for one visit. Send the list with photos and your move-in date, and we'll quote and schedule around it.

Can you fix interior doors that stick in an older house?

Usually. Most sticking doors come down to loose hinge screws, a shifted frame or a strike that no longer lines up. We start with the simple fixes and only plane the door if it's actually needed, then seal any bare edge.

Do you work in Thorncliffe Park and Flemingdon Park apartment buildings?

Yes. We follow building rules for elevators and working hours. If you rent, check with your landlord before mounting a TV or changing locks, since many leases cover both.

Can you help get a Leaside house ready to list?

Yes. Send us the realtor's or inspector's list, or walk us through what bothers you. We clear drywall dings, sticky doors, loose hardware and caulking in one organized visit, ideally before the photographer comes.
