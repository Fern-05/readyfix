Older houses that deserve careful repairs

York was a separate city until the 1998 amalgamation, and it's home to some of Toronto's most established streets. Around Oakwood Village, Fairbank, Caledonia, Silverthorn and Keelesdale, most homes are brick detached houses and semis from the first half of the twentieth century. Humewood–Cedarvale and Baby Point have larger homes from the same era. Weston has its own history, with older houses on tree-lined streets alongside apartment towers, and Mount Dennis has a similar mix.

Older houses here often still have plaster walls, original doors and trim, and a basement that's been finished, refinished or turned into a rental unit along the way. That means drilling has to be careful, doors and frames have settled out of square, and repairs need to blend with what's already there. On semis, we also keep in mind that the shared wall is your neighbour's wall too.

The work York homes need most

  • Drywall & plaster patching

    Old plaster cracks at corners and around doors as a house settles. We fill and tape cracks and patch holes so they're flush and ready to paint.

  • Doors & trim

    A century of settling leaves frames out of square. We adjust hinges and strikes, plane only what's needed, and re-secure loose trim.

  • Lock replacement

    Older doors don't always fit modern hardware without adjustment. We fit new deadbolts and handlesets properly and align the strike so it latches.

  • TV & artwork mounting

    Studs behind plaster and lath are harder to find, and some walls are solid brick. We locate solid backing and choose anchors for the wall, not the box.

  • Punch lists

    Turning over a basement unit or getting a semi ready to sell? We clear the whole list in one visit, with photos if you can't be there.

  • Small jobs

    Loose stair railings, sagging closet rods, cabinet doors and towel bars: the odd jobs every older house collects. One trip covers the lot.

FAQ

York handyman questions

Can you patch cracked plaster walls?

Yes. Small to medium cracks and holes in plaster are patched, taped where needed and primed so they're ready for paint. If the plaster has pulled away from the lath over a large area, patching won't last, and we'll tell you that plainly and explain the options.

Can you mount a TV on a plaster or brick wall?

Yes. On plaster, we locate the studs behind the lath and drill carefully to avoid cracking. On brick, we use masonry anchors set into solid brick rather than the mortar. If a wall won't hold the load safely, we'll suggest a better spot.

Do you work on shared walls in semi-detached houses?

Yes. We check what the shared wall is made of before drilling, use anchors that suit it, and keep the noisy part of the job short and within regular daytime hours.

Can you get a basement apartment ready for a new tenant?

Yes. Drywall patches, door and cabinet fixes, lock changes and general touch-ups are a good fit for one visit. Send the list with photos and the move-in date, and we'll quote and schedule around it.
