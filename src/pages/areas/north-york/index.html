Mid-century homes, new builds and condo towers

North York covers a lot of ground, and its homes range widely. Don Mills was laid out in the 1950s as one of Canada's first planned communities, and its low, mid-century houses still line the crescents. Willowdale, Bayview Village, Lawrence Manor, Bathurst Manor and Newtonbrook filled in with post-war bungalows and side-splits, and many of those lots now hold large new custom homes. Along Yonge Street around North York Centre and Sheppard, and near Bayview Village, condo towers keep going up.

That mix shapes the work. Newer houses bring tall walls, big windows and open-concept rooms that need TVs, mirrors and long curtain rods mounted properly, plus the nail pops and seam cracks that show up as new framing dries and settles. The older houses need doors, trim and cabinets brought back into line. The condos come with elevator bookings and building rules, which we handle the same way we do downtown.

What North York homes need most often

  • TV & artwork mounting

    Open-concept rooms and tall feature walls are made for big screens and gallery walls. We find the framing, mount level and keep the cables out of sight.

  • Curtain rod installation

    Wide windows need centre supports, and condo windows often need anchors into concrete. We set brackets so long rods stay straight under heavy drapes.

  • Drywall repair

    New framing shrinks as it dries, which pushes out nail pops and opens seams. We reset fasteners, re-tape where needed and leave each repair paint-ready.

  • Punch lists

    Whether you're selling a Bayview Village bungalow or moving into a Yonge Street condo, we work through the list in one organized visit.

  • Doors & trim

    New flooring changes door clearances, and older homes settle. We trim, rehang and adjust doors, then re-secure and caulk baseboard and casing.

  • Cabinet repair

    Mid-century kitchens and builder-grade cabinets both lose their hinges and slides in time. We replace and adjust them, and fit soft-close hardware if you want it.

FAQ

North York handyman questions

Can you mount a TV above a fireplace?

Often, yes, but it depends on what's behind the wall and how hot it gets above the firebox. We check the wall construction and the mount's rating before drilling, and we'll tell you plainly if the spot isn't a good idea. We keep cables tidy, but any new outlet or wiring behind the TV needs a licensed electrician.

Do you fix nail pops and cracks in newer homes?

Yes. They're common in the first couple of years as framing dries. We reset popped fasteners, repair cracked seams and prime each repair. If your home is still under the builder's warranty, check with the builder first.

Do you work in the condos along Yonge Street and Sheppard?

Yes. We confirm elevator bookings, protect hallways and floors, and keep dust down with HEPA extraction, the same way we work in downtown towers.

Can you hang long curtain rods across a wide window?

Yes. Long rods need a centre bracket or two, set into framing, a header or concrete depending on the wall. Tell us the window width and the curtain weight when you send photos, and we'll bring the right hardware.
