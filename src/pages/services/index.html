FAQ

Service questions

How far ahead should I book?

We keep open slots every week. Simple repairs usually book within 2–3 business days; larger scopes are scheduled within about a week.

Are materials included?

Yes, we stock common materials such as drywall, compound, caulking, fasteners, anchors and standard trim. Specialty finishes and hardware can be sourced at cost, or you can supply your own.

Can I combine different services in one visit?

Yes, and it's usually the most cost-effective way to book. Send your full list and we'll plan one visit to handle as much of it as possible. See how pricing works.

Do you work with property managers and realtors?

Absolutely. We coordinate key pickup and tenant access, send before and after photos, and can invoice centrally.

Is there anything you don't do?

We don't take on work that requires a licensed electrician, plumber or gas fitter, or large renovations. If your list includes those, we'll tell you upfront and can coordinate around the trade you hire.
