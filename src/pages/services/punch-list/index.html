Common problems

Typical punch list items, and how we fix them

  • Inspection report items

    Inspectors often flag loose handrails, failed caulking, sticking doors and damaged trim. We go through the report line by line, mark which items we handle and which need a licensed trade, and take care of the rest.

  • Caulking and grout

    Cracked or mouldy caulk around tubs, showers and counters is cut out completely, the joint is cleaned and dried, and a fresh bead of mildew-resistant silicone goes on. Crumbling grout lines are refreshed.

  • Wall dings and old holes

    Anchor holes, scuffs, nail pops and dented corners are filled, sanded and primed so the walls are ready for a quick coat of paint before photos, showings or move-in day.

  • Doors that won't latch

    Usually a sagging hinge or a strike that's drifted out of line. We drive longer hinge screws into the framing, adjust the strike, and plane or shim only where it's needed.

  • Loose hardware and fixtures

    Towel bars, paper holders, cabinet pulls and closet rods that wobble are re-secured, with stripped screw holes filled or re-anchored so they stay tight instead of loosening again.

  • Move-outs and turnovers

    Between tenants or before a listing, we work through the wear-and-tear items and photograph each finished fix, so landlords and realtors have a record without driving over.

FAQ

Punch list questions

Can you work from a home inspection report?

Yes. Send the report or your realtor's list and we'll go through it item by item, confirm what we can handle, and flag anything that needs a licensed electrician, plumber or roofer so you know who else to call.

How quickly can you finish a list before a closing?

Simple lists usually book within 2–3 days, and we keep rush slots for closing-critical work. Tell us your closing, listing or move-in date when you send the list so we can plan around it.

Do I need to be home for the visit?

No. Many punch lists are for owners, landlords and realtors who aren't on site. We coordinate access with tenants, concierge or your realtor and send before-and-after photos of each item when we're done.

Do you bring materials?

We bring common caulking, anchors, screws and patching materials. Specialty hardware, matching trim or replacement parts can be sourced at cost, or you can supply your own.

Do you work in condos and occupied rentals?

Yes. We confirm elevator bookings with management, protect floors and common areas, and work around tenants' schedules, keeping noise and dust to a minimum for neighbours.

What should I prepare before the visit?

A list or report with the room for each item, any replacement hardware you've already bought, and access to every room on the list. Clearing a little space around each item saves time on the day.
