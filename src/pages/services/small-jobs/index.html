Common problems

Common small jobs, and how we fix them

  • Hardware that keeps loosening

    Handles, hinges and hooks that work loose usually have stripped screw holes. We fill and re-drill, or move to fresh material, so they hold instead of just being re-tightened.

  • Towel bars pulling out

    Bathroom accessories often hang on plastic plugs in drywall. We re-anchor them into studs or with proper hollow-wall anchors, and patch the old holes.

  • Closet doors off track

    Bi-folds that jump the track get their pivots adjusted and new guides where needed. Sliders get their rollers and floor guides set so they glide and close evenly.

  • Holes and nail pops

    Picture holes, dents and popped screws are filled, sanded and primed so they're ready for a quick touch of paint. Bigger damage is handled by our drywall repair service.

  • Tired caulking

    Cracked caulk around tubs, sinks, counters and trim is removed and re-run with the right product for the spot: silicone in wet areas, paintable caulk on trim.

  • Everything else on the list

    Door stops, cabinet bumpers, smoke alarm batteries, hooks and small shelves: the things that aren't big enough for their own trip. If it's on your list, ask.

FAQ

Small jobs questions

Is there a minimum?

Yes, we book small jobs with a simple minimum. Send your list and we'll confirm pricing before arrival. The more items you bundle into one visit, the more you get out of it.

Do you bring materials?

We bring common fasteners, caulk and patching supplies. Specialty parts can be sourced at cost or provided by you.

Can you add jobs on site?

Often, yes, if time allows. We'll confirm any additional cost before going ahead.

Are there jobs you don't do?

Anything that needs a licensed electrician, plumber or gas fitter, such as installing light fixtures or outlets, replacing faucets or connecting gas appliances. We'll tell you if something on your list falls into that category.

Can you handle small jobs at a rental or while I'm away?

Yes. We coordinate access with tenants or concierge and send photo updates by text or email, which works well for landlords, property managers and remote owners.

How should I prepare for the visit?

Have any parts you've already bought on hand, clear a little space around each item, and let us know about parking or condo elevator bookings ahead of time so we can confirm them with management.
