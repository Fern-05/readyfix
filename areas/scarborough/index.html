Bungalows, backsplits and townhouse complexes

Scarborough is big, and most of it was built out in the decades after the war. Wexford, Clairlea, Dorset Park and Bendale have streets of brick bungalows. Further north and east, in Agincourt, L'Amoreaux, Milliken, Malvern and West Hill, you'll find backsplits, side-splits and the townhouse complexes that went up through the 1960s, '70s and '80s. Down by the Bluffs, Birch Cliff, Cliffside and Guildwood mix older homes with post-war houses, and high-rises cluster around Scarborough Town Centre and the main corridors.

Many Scarborough houses have a basement apartment, and many townhouses and condos are rented out, so a lot of the work here is about keeping units in good shape between tenants, getting a house ready to sell, or clearing the list after you move in. That's punch-list work, and it's what ReadyFix is built for.

Where we help most in Scarborough

  • Punch lists

    Turnovers, sales and move-ins all come with a list. We work through it room by room in one visit and send photos so you can check it off remotely.

  • Drywall repair

    Basement units take the most wear. Once a leak is fixed, we cut out damaged board, replace it, stain-block and leave it ready for paint.

  • Lock replacement

    New owners and landlords both want fresh locks. We swap deadbolts and handlesets and line up the strike so the door latches cleanly.

  • Doors & trim

    Backsplits and side-splits have plenty of interior doors on short stair runs. We fix the ones that rub, drift or won't latch, and re-secure loose trim.

  • Cabinet repair

    Rental kitchens see hard use. New hinges and drawer slides fix most sagging doors and sticky drawers without replacing the cabinets.

  • Small jobs

    Bifold doors off the track, wobbly railings, loose towel bars and missing doorstops. Small on their own, worth doing together.

FAQ

Scarborough handyman questions

Do you work in Scarborough condo townhouse complexes?

Yes. One thing to check first: in many condo townhouses, exterior doors, windows and some fixtures are common elements that the condo corporation looks after. Check with management before booking work on those. Anything inside your unit, we can quote right away.

Can you change the locks between tenants?

Yes. We replace deadbolts and handlesets with hardware you choose or that we source at cost, and make sure every door latches and locks smoothly. We don't do emergency lockouts.

Scarborough is big. How soon can you get here?

Scarborough is part of our regular Toronto service area, so the same timing applies: most jobs are booked within 2–3 business days, and we reply to quote requests within one business day.

Can you fix basement drywall after a leak?

Yes, once the source of the leak has been repaired. We remove wet or damaged board, let the area dry, then replace, tape, finish and stain-block it so marks don't bleed through the paint.
