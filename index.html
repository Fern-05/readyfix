FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can you come out?

Most jobs are booked within 2–3 business days, and we keep a few rush slots each week for closing-critical or urgent repairs. We reply to quote requests within one business day.

Do you have a minimum job size?

No job is too small to ask about. Small items are often best bundled into one visit with the rest of your list, which keeps the cost per item down. See how pricing works.

Do you bring materials?

Yes. We carry common materials like drywall, compound, caulking, fasteners and anchors. Specialty items such as matching hardware or trim profiles can be sourced at cost, or you can supply your own.

Can you work while I'm not home?

Yes. Many landlords, realtors and owners arrange key or lockbox access. We send before and after photos so you can see the finished work.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Toronto, including downtown, East York, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough and York, plus Mississauga and Markham. See all service areas.
