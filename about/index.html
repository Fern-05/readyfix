What ReadyFix is

ReadyFix Handyman Services is a Toronto handyman business focused on repairs, fixes and punch lists. Not full renovations or new kitchens: the drywall hole from a doorknob, the door that won't latch, the cabinet hinge that gave out, the TV still leaning against the wall, and the list of small items an inspector or realtor hands you a week before closing.

Those jobs are hard to get done in Toronto. Larger contractors are set up for big projects, so small repairs wait weeks for a call back, or never get one. We built ReadyFix around exactly that kind of work, so we can quote it quickly, show up when we said we would, and finish the list in as few visits as possible.

We also know where our work ends. Anything that needs a licensed electrician or plumber, we leave to them. When they've opened up a wall or ceiling to do their part, we're happy to come in afterwards and close it up properly.

How we work

What you can expect on every job

None of this is complicated. It's just what we'd want from someone working in our own home.

  • Confirmed arrival windows

    Most jobs are booked within 2–3 business days. We text when we're on the way, and we show up when we said we would.

  • Clear quotes, no surprises

    You get a written price and approve it before we start. If we find something extra, we talk to you about it first.

  • Stocked for the job

    Drywall, fast-set compound, anchors, caulking and specialty tools are on the truck, so jobs rarely wait on a store run.

  • Clean work zones

    Floor protection and HEPA dust control, which matter most in condos and occupied homes.

  • Photo updates

    Before and after photos for landlords, realtors and owners who can't be on site, plus simple online invoicing.

  • Insured & accountable

    ReadyFix is insured, and every job starts with a clear written quote, so you know exactly what you're agreeing to.

Simple from start to finish

    You get a written price and a choice of arrival windows, usually within one business day.
