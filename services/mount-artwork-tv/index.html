Mounting FAQs

Do you provide brackets?

We can supply common TV brackets or install the one you provide. We verify compatibility and weight rating.

Can you hide the wires?

We offer cord covers or, where feasible, in-wall routing using code-compliant methods. We’ll confirm options on site.

Is condo work okay?

Yes. We protect floors, mind noise rules, and clean thoroughly after drilling.
