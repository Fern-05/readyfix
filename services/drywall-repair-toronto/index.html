Seamless drywall patches, done in one visit

A good drywall repair is one you can't find afterwards. That takes more than a dab of filler: the damaged area is cut back to solid board, patched, taped, and feathered wide so the repair sits flush with the wall around it. Then we match the existing texture, sand, and prime so it's ready for paint.

Common problems

What causes drywall damage, and how we fix it

  • Holes and dents

    Small holes get filled and feathered. Larger holes get a new piece of board screwed to backing, taped and mudded in several thin coats so the patch doesn't show.

  • Access cut-outs

    After a plumber or electrician opens a wall or ceiling, we close it properly: square the opening, add backing, install new board and finish it to match.

  • Water damage

    Once the leak is fixed and the area is dry, soft or stained board is cut out and replaced, then primed with a stain-blocking primer so marks don't bleed through paint.

  • Cracks and seams

    Recurring cracks usually mean failed tape. We open the joint, re-tape with the right tape for the location, and feather it wide so it stays closed.

  • Nail and screw pops

    Popped fasteners are reset and secured with new screws beside them, then spotted and sanded flush. Common in newer homes as framing settles.

  • Ceilings and texture

    Ceiling patches are the hardest to hide. We blend orange peel, knockdown or smooth finishes so the repair disappears under paint and light.

Our process

How we repair drywall

  1. Protect the area

    Drop sheets, floor protection and a HEPA vacuum at the source keep dust contained, even in occupied condos.

  2. Cut back & patch

    Damaged board is cut back to solid material. New board is fastened to backing so the patch is solid, not just filled.

  3. Tape, mud & texture

    Seams are taped and finished in thin coats with fast-setting compound, then textured to match the surrounding surface.

  4. Sand, prime & clean

    We sand flush, prime the repair and clean up, leaving the area ready for paint, often the same day.

FAQ

Drywall repair questions

Can you match existing texture like orange peel or knockdown?

Yes. We replicate light orange peel, knockdown and smooth finishes with spray texture and hand tools, then sand for a flush surface so the repair blends in once painted.

Do you repair drywall in downtown Toronto condos?

Yes, condo work is a big part of what we do. We confirm elevator bookings with management, protect hallways and floors, and keep noise and dust to a minimum for neighbours.

What size of holes can you fix?

Everything from nail pops to large cut-outs around 4×4 feet. Anything larger becomes a full board replacement, which we also handle.

How long before the wall can be painted?

With fast-setting compounds we usually finish sanding and priming the same day. Painting can typically follow within 24 hours.

Do you paint after the repair?

We prime every repair so it's paint-ready. Touch-up painting of the patched area can be added to the quote; for full walls or rooms we're happy to coordinate with your painter.

Can you help after a leak?

Yes. Once the source of the leak is fixed, we remove wet or damaged board, let the cavity dry, then replace, finish and stain-block the area. Same-day help to open up and dry out a wet ceiling is often available when leaks happen.
