Common problems

Common cabinet problems, and how we fix them

  • Sagging doors

    Most concealed hinges have three adjustments: side to side, height and depth. Often a door only needs re-tuning. If a hinge is worn or cracked, we replace it with a matching one.

  • Stripped hinge screws

    When screws pull out of particleboard, a bigger screw won't hold for long. We fill and re-drill the holes with hardwood dowels or a repair insert so the hinge has something solid to grip.

  • Sticky or dropping drawers

    Worn rollers, bent side-mount slides or broken undermount clips make drawers grind or fall. We measure the drawer and box and fit matching replacement slides, including soft-close options.

  • Slamming doors

    Soft-close hinges or add-on dampers stop the bang without new doors. We fit them to your existing hinge cups where possible and adjust so every door closes evenly.

  • Loose drawer fronts

    Fronts that wobble or have pulled off are re-fastened from inside the box and adjusted so the gaps line up with the doors and drawers around them.

  • Hardware refresh

    New pulls are drilled with a jig so they line up across every door and drawer. If the new hole spacing differs from the old, we plan placement to cover or fill the old holes cleanly.

Our process

How we repair cabinets

  1. Diagnose

    We check each door and drawer, identify the hinge and slide types, and confirm which parts can be adjusted and which need replacing.

  2. Protect & prep

    Counters and floors are covered, and doors or drawers are removed and labelled so everything goes back in the right place.

  3. Repair & replace

    Stripped holes are repaired, worn hardware is swapped, and new pulls are drilled with a jig for consistent placement.

  4. Align & test

    Every door is adjusted for even gaps and every drawer is cycled open and closed before we clean up and walk you through it.

FAQ

Cabinet repair questions

Can you fix my cabinets without replacing them?

In most cases, yes. Cabinet boxes usually outlast their hinges and slides, so adjusting or replacing hardware, repairing stripped screw holes and re-securing drawer fronts solves most problems. If a box or door is water-damaged or delaminating, we'll tell you plainly what's worth fixing.

Can you add soft-close to my existing cabinets?

Usually. Many hinge lines have soft-close versions that fit the existing cup holes, and add-on dampers work on others. Drawers can be upgraded with soft-close slides sized to the box. A photo of your current hinges and slides helps us confirm what fits.

Can you match my existing hinges or handles?

Yes. We source matching hardware or install what you provide, matching finish and drill patterns to avoid visible extra holes. A photo of an open hinge, or any brand stamp on it, helps us find the right part.

Do you repair cabinets in condos?

Yes, condo kitchens are a regular part of our work. We protect counters and common areas, confirm elevator bookings with management, and coordinate access with concierge as needed.

Should I buy the hardware myself?

Either works. We can recommend hinges and slides for your cabinet line and source them at cost, or install the exact set you've already purchased. If you're buying your own, send us photos first and we'll tell you what to measure, like drawer depth and hinge overlay, so it fits the first time.

How long does a cabinet repair take?

Tuning a few doors is quick. Replacing hinges or slides throughout a kitchen takes longer, and we'll give you a time estimate with your quote. We aim to finish cabinet jobs in a single visit.
