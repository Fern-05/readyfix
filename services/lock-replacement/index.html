New locks that work smoothly every time

A new lock is only as good as its fit. If the bolt drags on the strike, the latch doesn't fully catch, or you have to push the door to lock it, the hardware wears faster and the door isn't as secure as it should be. Most problems after a lock swap come down to alignment, not the lock itself.

ReadyFix replaces locks and door hardware for homeowners, landlords and condo owners across Toronto. We install hardware you've bought or source trusted hardware at cost, adjust bores and mortises so it sits flush, align the strike, and test the keys, thumbturn and any smart features with you. Common problems

Common lock and latch problems, and how we fix them

  • Deadbolt drags or won't throw

    The bolt is usually hitting the strike because the door has sagged. We tighten the hinges into the framing, then adjust the strike or deepen its bore so the bolt throws fully without force.

  • Latch won't catch

    We reposition the strike plate or adjust its lip, and fill the old screw holes so the plate sits flat and tight and the door closes with light pressure.

  • New lock, old holes

    New hardware doesn't always match the old prep. We check backset, bore size and door thickness, and adjust the edge bore or mortise so the new lock sits flush and square.

  • Weak strike plates

    Short strike screws only reach the door jamb. Where the hardware allows, we fasten the strike with longer screws into the framing behind it, so the door is harder to force.

  • Smart lock installs

    We fit the smart deadbolt, adjust the latch depth and door alignment so the motor isn't straining, and test pairing and app connectivity with you before we leave.

  • Rental turnovers

    Between tenants, landlords often swap entry hardware. We replace the locks, test every key, and send photos for your records. For re-keying existing cylinders, we coordinate with a locksmith.

Our process

How we replace your locks

  1. Confirm the fit

    We check door thickness, backset and bore size so the lock you've picked fits, or recommend hardware that does.

  2. Remove & prep

    Old hardware comes off, finishes are protected, and bores or mortises are adjusted where the new lock needs it.

  3. Install & align

    The new lock goes in, the strike is aligned, and hinges are tightened so the door latches without force.

  4. Test with you

    We test keys, thumbturn, latch and any smart features with you, then clear away the old hardware.

FAQ

Lock replacement questions

Do you supply the locks?

We can install locks you provide or source trusted hardware at cost. If you're buying your own, send us a photo of the door edge first and we'll tell you what to look for.

Can you re-key my locks or help if I'm locked out?

No, those are locksmith services. We can coordinate re-keying with a locksmith, or install new locks keyed alike when available so one key works on every door. If you're locked out, call a locksmith first; once you're in, we can replace the hardware.

Can you change the lock on my condo suite door?

Usually, yes. Many condo suite doors are fire-rated, and some buildings have rules about entry hardware or need a key for management, so check with your property manager first. We'll install hardware that suits the door and coordinate access with concierge.

Can you install a smart lock?

Yes. Most smart deadbolts fit a standard deadbolt bore. We check door thickness and backset, adjust the door so the bolt throws without strain (which also helps battery life), and test the lock and app with you.

Will my door close better afterwards?

Yes. We adjust hinges and strikes so the door latches smoothly with your new hardware, and we don't leave until it closes and locks without pushing.

How do I know which lock fits my door?

Measure the backset (door edge to the centre of the handle hole; most residential doors are 2-3/8 or 2-3/4 inches) and the door thickness, and note whether the handle and deadbolt are separate or one handleset. Or just send photos and we'll confirm.
