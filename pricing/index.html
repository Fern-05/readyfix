How much does a handyman cost in Toronto?

The honest answer is that it depends on the job, and anyone who gives you a price before seeing the work is guessing. A small drywall patch in a house is a different job from the same patch on a textured condo ceiling with an elevator to book. Two doors that need a hinge screw are different from two doors that need to be taken down and trimmed.

So we don't publish a one-size-fits-all rate card. Instead, we quote every job from photos, in writing, before any work starts. Quotes are free, and most are back to you within one business day.

How quotes work

From photos to a fixed plan in four steps

  1. Send photos

    Text or email a picture of each item, plus a short description. A list is perfect. If something can't be priced from photos, we'll tell you what we need.

  2. Get a written quote

    We reply with a price for the work and a choice of arrival windows, usually within one business day.

  3. Approve and book

    Once you approve the quote, we book your arrival window. If you want to add or drop items, we update the quote first.

  4. Work, walk-through, invoice

    We do the work, review it with you (or send photos if you're away) and invoice online. Anything outside the quote is discussed before we do it.

What affects the price

Why two similar jobs can cost different amounts

These are the things we look at when we put a quote together. Good photos help us account for all of them up front.

  • Size and scope

    A nail-hole fill is not a cut-out patch. Bigger repairs need more material, more coats and more drying time between them.

  • Number of items

    Set-up, protection and clean-up happen once per visit. The more items share that visit, the lower the cost of each one.

  • Materials and hardware

    Common materials come with us. Specialty items, like matching hinges, a particular lockset or a trim profile, depend on what you choose.

  • Access and building logistics

    Condo elevator bookings, loading rules, high ceilings that need a tall ladder, and tight or furnished spaces all affect how long a job takes.

  • Wall and surface type

    Drywall over wood studs is quickest. Concrete, brick, steel studs and old plaster need different anchors, tools and more care.

  • Finish and texture matching

    Matching orange peel or knockdown texture, or a repair on a ceiling where light shows everything, takes extra steps to disappear.

Bundle and save

Why one visit with five items beats five visits with one

Every visit has a fixed part: travel, parking, carrying tools in, laying floor protection, setting up dust control and cleaning up at the end. In a condo, add the elevator booking. That part costs about the same whether we fix one thing or ten.

When several items share one visit, that fixed part is spread across all of them, so the cost per item drops. It's also less disruption for you: one arrival window, one clean-up, one invoice.

That's why we always ask for the whole list. Even small items that wouldn't be worth a trip on their own make sense once we're already there.

FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does a handyman cost in Toronto?

It depends on the size of the job, the number of items, materials, access and the finish required. The quickest way to a real number is to text or email photos of each item. We'll send a free written quote, usually within one business day.

Are quotes free?

Yes. Most jobs can be quoted from photos and a short description. If something can't be priced that way, we'll tell you what else we need.

What if the job turns out bigger than expected?

We stop and talk to you before doing anything outside the quote. Sometimes opening a wall or taking a door down shows something photos couldn't. You decide whether to add it, and nothing extra is done or billed without your approval.

Is there a minimum job size?

No job is too small to ask about. Small items are usually best bundled into one visit with the rest of your list, which keeps the cost per item down.

Do I pay for materials separately?

Your written quote shows what's included. We carry common materials like drywall, compound, caulking, fasteners and anchors. Specialty items such as matching hardware or trim profiles can be sourced at cost, or you can supply your own.

Does working in a condo cost more?

It can take more time. Elevator bookings, hallway protection, loading rules and building hours all add steps, and drilling into concrete is slower than drywall. We factor that into the quote up front, so there's no surprise on the day.

How do I pay?

We invoice online once the work is done and you've seen it, in person or through photos. The invoice matches your approved quote, plus anything extra you agreed to along the way.
